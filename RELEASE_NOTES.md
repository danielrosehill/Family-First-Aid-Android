Sideloadable APK. Download the `.apk` below on an Android phone and open it (allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when prompted). It installs over the previous version and keeps your review progress.

**New in 0.3.0**

- **Flowcharts tab**: every protocol as a chart of its decision forks, plus a full map of the "What's happening?" guide.
- **Resources tab**: phone numbers, all 27 Terem urgent-care branches (address, hours, branch page, map), a Hebrew glossary of 77 emergency words and phrases with audio, and the review schedule.
- Topic pages fold equipment, source and progress into accordions. Long pages have a pinned "Jump to" bar.
- Removed two Jerusalem Terem entries that are no longer listed on terem.com.

Not an official medical tool. In an emergency, call 101.
